# Product photos

The starter catalog uses photos from `public/products/`. Each file is named after the product slug and was
converted to a square WebP (about 1000px) so pages stay fast.

## Please read before you launch
These photos were supplied as ready-made images from the internet. I cannot confirm that you own them or may use
them commercially. Many of them show **other companies' brand names, labels, watermarks or advertising text**
(for example on jars, bags and boxes). On a live store this can mislead customers, because the product in the photo
is not the one you will deliver, and it can break the photo owners' rights.

**Before the real launch, replace them with your own photos** of your own packaging. The easiest way:
Admin > Products > open a product > Upload from device. Uploaded photos are never replaced by the starter photos.

## Left out on purpose
- *Hunza Mountain Green Tea*: the supplied image is another seller's advert with their phone number and social
  handles, so customers could contact the wrong business. The product shows the built-in illustration instead.
- `images.jpg` (a flyer with another shop's prices), `gift box demo` (carries another shop's watermark) and
  `gift boxs demo` (shows other companies' products) were not used.

## How the order is chosen
Products with the cleanest, sharpest, most attractive photos are listed first and the twelve best are marked as
best sellers, so the home page opens with them. Products showing adverts or posters come last.

## Applying to a database that already has products
Admin > Products > **Apply starter photos**. It only changes products that still show an illustration.
